Founder & Longevity Coach

Meet Venus Davis

Venus Davis is a Longevity Coach and the founder of The Strong Academy, the DMV's community for EverSTRONG and TeamSTRONG. A native New Yorker with deep roots in service, her journey began in the discipline of the U.S. Air Force, followed by a fast-paced corporate career while raising her son. That lived experience of prioritizing everything but her own well-being became the catalyst for her life's work.

With over a decade of credentials including Collegiate Athletic Trainer, ACE Certified Personal Trainer, Functional Movement Coach, and USA Weightlifting Coach, Venus translates complex science into accessible, empowering strength. She built EverSTRONG to bridge the gap between fitness and medicine for adults who want strength that ages as well as they do. TeamSTRONG extends that same energy to all ages and abilities.

Venus is driven by a belief that your strongest decades should still be ahead of you, and that real strength comes from training, knowledge, and a community that shows up. When she's not coaching, you'll find her watching sci-fi, training for her own next challenge, or dreaming up ways to serve her community.

Her mantra is simple: Be Well. Stay Committed. Train for Life.
